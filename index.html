About the 2026 Nepal-Tibet floods ↗
✓ What ReachAloud is

The last-mile layer that turns an emergency alert into clear, spoken voice in any language, so people who can't read the screen still understand it. It works offline once loaded and needs no account to hear the demo alerts.

○ What it is not

It does not detect disasters, send alerts, or replace official warning systems. The warning still comes from authorities and sensors - ReachAloud makes that warning understandable to everyone it reaches.
